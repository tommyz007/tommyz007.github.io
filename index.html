Paper Lab Evidence Collector
Privacy Terms

Private research utility

Evidence collection with a deliberately narrow boundary.

Paper Lab Evidence Collector is a single-user local research tool. It gathers public SEC filings and selected issuer-alert emails for human review. It does not place trades, approve decisions, or connect to a broker.

Gmail access is read-only. The collector requests only the Gmail scope needed to view messages and settings. It cannot send, modify, archive, or delete mail.

Bounded collection

Only messages matching configured issuer-alert searches are examined, with explicit lookback and message-count limits.

Minimal retention

Raw message bodies and attachments are not persisted. The local archive contains only normalized research evidence and provenance.

Local operation

OAuth credentials, tokens, archives, reports, and operational logs stay on the authorized user's computer.

Human decisions

New or revised evidence remains unapproved until a person reviews it. Collection never changes trading state.
