Paper Lab Evidence Collector
Privacy Terms

Effective October 15, 2026

Privacy policy

Paper Lab Evidence Collector is a private, single-user research utility operated locally by the Google account holder who authorizes it.

Google data accessed

The application requests the read-only Gmail scope that permits viewing email messages and settings. It verifies the authorized mailbox, searches for configured issuer-alert messages, and reads only bounded matching results.

How data is used

Message metadata, a source link, a short excerpt, timestamps, and integrity information may be normalized into a local research archive for evidence review. Google user data is used only to provide this user-facing research function.

Storage and protection

  • Raw email bodies and attachments are not persisted.
  • OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in owner-only local files.
  • Normalized evidence, run manifests, and reports remain on the authorized user's computer.
  • The public website contains no analytics, advertising, cookies, tracking pixels, account system, or uploaded mailbox data.

Sharing and sale

Google user data is not sold, shared with advertisers, transferred to data brokers, or used to train generalized machine-learning models. The application does not disclose Google user data to third parties except when required by law.

Retention and deletion

Normalized research records follow a local retention policy. The user can remove the local archive and token files, and can revoke the application's access at any time from the Google Account connections page.

Scope limitations

The application cannot send, edit, archive, or delete Gmail messages. It has no broker, order-entry, or trading-database access and cannot approve or execute trading decisions.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the user-support address displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
